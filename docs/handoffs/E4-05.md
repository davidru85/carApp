## Ready Check

- Backlog story: `E4-05` (`docs/BACKLOG.md`, story section and index row).
- Acceptance criteria reviewed:
  - Settings shows a "link account" row only while the session is `ANONYMOUS`; never for
    `PERMANENT`; hidden while `LOCAL` (`D-224`);
  - Android offers Google; iOS offers Apple and Google (`docs/SPECIFICATION.md §7 F-1`);
  - the host starts with `startAccountConversion(provider)`, runs the native picker that `E4-01`
    refactored and completes through `completeGoogleSignIn` or `completeAppleSignIn`; it never calls
    `startPermanentSignIn` for a conversion and contains no conversion logic;
  - a successful link keeps the UID and every vehicle and fuel entry; the session becomes
    `PERMANENT`, the row disappears and the reminders stop;
  - `AuthError.CredentialAlreadyInUse` shows the destructive confirmation of
    `docs/CONTRACTS.md §11.3`; cancelling leaves the anonymous session and all data untouched;
    confirming calls `confirmAccountConversion(Confirmation.AdoptExistingAccount)`, and the host
    renders the progress, the typed failures and the `E2-04` resume behaviour;
  - every conversion `UiMessage` code and confirmation has Spanish and English copy.
- Dependencies checked: `E4-01` is complete on this branch (the Settings screen and the refactored
  Google coordinators); `E2-04` and `E3-11` are merged.
- Decisions checked: `D-210` and `D-224` are `Accepted`; applies `D-62`, `D-145` and `D-151` to
  `D-154`. The row and collision-confirmation design was approved by the owner on 2026-10-16
  (non-normative, no decision ID). No dependent decision is `Proposed` or `Pending`.
- Facts verified at intake:
  - `startAccountConversion` publishes `AuthError.ProviderUnavailable` unless the session is
    anonymous (`shared/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/ruizurraca/carapp/StateHolders.kt:279-294`);
  - `confirmAccountConversion` returns early for any confirmation other than
    `AdoptExistingAccount` (`StateHolders.kt:296-297`);
  - a displayed collision is cancelled with `clearMessage()`, which drops the in-memory credential
    and reports `AccountConversionFailed(CANCELLED)` (`StateHolders.kt:383-402`, pinned by
    `SessionStateHolderTest`); `docs/CONTRACTS.md §20.10` did not state it, because it named
    `confirmAccountConversion`, which ignores every confirmation other than `AdoptExistingAccount`
    (`StateHolders.kt:296-298`). The gap was escalated as `E405-Q1`; the owner chose option A on
    2026-10-16, recorded as `D-227` by the next `E4-05` commit. A cancelled native picker is
    `failSignIn(NativeSignInFailure.CANCELLED)` (`StateHolders.kt:235-258`);
  - a replacement interrupted after the session switch resumes from the durable marker (`D-151`)
    with no owner action, from the graph's auth collector and from every sync preflight
    (`AppGraph.kt:152-170, 264-270`); the host then shows `PERMANENT` with no message and the
    vehicle list's waiting indicator until the rows are rebuilt, and needs no state of its own. An
    interruption before the switch leaves the marker while the session is still anonymous: the
    credential is lost (`D-154`), backup fails until the owner links again, and the retry reuses the
    first snapshot. That hazard and a failed resume after the switch are accepted residual risks
    (`D-228`, `E405-Q2` option A) owned by `E2-10`;
  - conversion can publish `CONFIRMATION.AdoptExistingAccount`, `AUTH.PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE`,
    `AUTH.NETWORK_UNAVAILABLE`, `AUTH.NO_ACCOUNT_AVAILABLE`, `AUTH.CANCELLED`, `AUTH.UNKNOWN`,
    `AUTH.UID_WOULD_CHANGE`, `AUTH.PERMISSION_DENIED`, `AUTH.CREDENTIAL_ALREADY_IN_USE`,
    `AUTH.REQUIRES_RECENT_LOGIN`, `AUTH.ACCOUNT_DELETION_REMOTE_FAILED` and the seven `REMOTE.*`
    codes. Before `E4-05`, `AUTH.UID_WOULD_CHANGE`, `AUTH.PERMISSION_DENIED`,
    `AUTH.CREDENTIAL_ALREADY_IN_USE` and `AUTH.UNKNOWN` fell to the generic fallback on both hosts and
    every `REMOTE.*` code read as a failed restore; `E4-05` gives each of them explicit copy.
- Normative sections reviewed: `docs/SPECIFICATION.md §3.1`, `§7 F-1`, `F-4`;
  `docs/CONTRACTS.md §11.3`, `§16.1` (conversion events), `§20.2` (`Confirmation`), `§20.10`
